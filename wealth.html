6. Control Rules

Equities incl. Commodities: 25–65%. Fixed Income – Bonds: 15–60%. Alternatives / Hedge Funds: 0–40%. Foreign Currencies: 0–70%. Money Market: 0–60%. Eligible Investment Universe: PASS. Concentration Limit: PASS.

Wealth methodology: latest Wealth / Wealth Strategy weights are read from Capital_Allocations. Trade effects are calculated against the fixed Wealth NAV of €356,059,009. Every proposal sheet is aggregated into Wealth. Existing security pricing uses HSBC where available and Yahoo Finance as fallback. Priced securities not explicitly identified as Fixed Income or Alternatives default to Equities incl. Commodities so the invested-asset and Money Market legs reconcile.

Foreign Currency: current exposure is provisionally derived from the uploaded HSBC positions and divided by the Wealth NAV. This can later be replaced by a consolidated Wealth foreign-currency source.
