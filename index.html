6. Control Rules

Equities incl. Commodities: 25–65%. Fixed Income – Bonds: 15–60%. Alternatives / Hedge Funds: 0–40%. Foreign Currencies: 0–70%. Money Market: 0–60%. Eligible Investment Universe: PASS. Concentration Limit: PASS. All seven controls must PASS for an account to PASS.

Valuation hierarchy: existing securities use HSBC EOP value per unit, then HSBC SOP value per unit. If HSBC cannot price the ISIN, the app calls the Yahoo Finance pricing service and converts the quote to EUR. For a completely new ISIN, Yahoo metadata is used where available; uncertain classifications remain REVIEW and prevent final PASS. Order settlement is assumed to occur in the order currency, so a security-to-cash trade does not by itself change total non-EUR exposure.
